Privacy Policy

Naya Automation (the "App") · Last updated: October 11, 2026

Naya Automation is an application that helps the Instagram account @nayaon_ig publish content and respond to comments and direct messages. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Information we receive

When someone comments on a post or sends a direct message to @nayaon_ig, Instagram sends us, through Meta's official API: the text of the comment or message, the sender's Instagram-scoped user ID, and a timestamp. We do not receive passwords, payment details, or private data beyond what Instagram provides for these events.

How we use information

  • To generate and send an automated reply to the comment or message.
  • To keep a basic log of interactions (time, type, sender ID, message text, reply text) so the account owner can review engagement.

Message text may be processed by an AI language model service solely to draft a reply. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the App (for example, hosting and AI processing providers), and only to the extent needed to provide the service. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Retention

Interaction logs are kept only as long as needed to operate the account and are deleted on request.

Data deletion

You can ask us to delete any information we hold about you by emailing mahiabhinav5@gmail.com with your Instagram username. We will delete it within 30 days. You can also remove the App's access to your data from your Instagram settings.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post changes on this page with a new date.
